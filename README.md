**VELORA BAY** is an original open-world crime game for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty (gta6-01). You are Jax Corvo, a wheelman dragged back into Velora Bay — a sun-soaked coastal city of neon boardwalks, rival crews and one last score. Steal and drive cars, outrun the Velora Bay Police (wanted level 0–5, helicopter at 3+), fight the Reyes crew, and play 8 story missions from Marco's garage to the Marina Vault. On foot and by car, with traffic, pedestrians, shops, a garage, radio stations, a HUD with a rotating minimap, and save/load.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests — `game/` is plain static files (three.js vendored under `game/vendor/three/`).

Controls: WASD move/drive · mouse drag to look · E enter/exit car or shop · click punch/shoot · 1 fists · 2 pistol · Q radio · P pause · M mute.
